How this comparison is made

Three different kinds of statement.

Scenario: a condensed, original paraphrase of a named section in AI 2027 or AI 2040. Fictional companies such as OpenBrain and DeepCent are not treated as real organizations. Where one scenario has no matching year, its actual narrative period is shown.

Observation: a dated release, publication, legal change, announcement, or incident with a direct source. A report being published is an event; the report's projections are not observed outcomes. Company benchmarks remain company-reported. Investment commitments are not completed construction.

Mapping: our editorial explanation of how that observation bears on a particular scenario mechanism. A precursor is not confirmation of an endpoint. No automated truth score, probability update, or percentage-complete metric is assigned.

Read the counterevidence, too.

METR's early-2025 developer slowdown is explicitly historical and paired with its February 2026 update. Stanford's employment results are descriptive, not a causal estimate. AISI's incident involved deliberately permissive evaluation settings; it is not presented as a sandbox escape or normal consumer behavior.

Daily actuals checks, not invented daily news.

Content snapshot dated loading. The latest dated event included is loading. A daily check can find no qualifying additions; it does not redate existing events or prove nothing happened. This remains a curated record, not a complete news archive.

Both site timelines use a single daily workflow at 7:00 am Sydney time. New companion entries require their own reviewed scenario connection and verified original source. The published check status and content-change time are separate; failed runs retain the last good record. An overdue check is labelled rather than silently called fresh. The original scenario text and timing are not automatically rewritten.

Event dates and source publication dates are separate. Revisions and effective dates are identified. Month-only dates remain month-only and are grouped within their month, not assigned a fabricated day. An undated policy page is labelled as such. Opening this page does not refresh the research.

Sources were read through public web retrieval or a browser. Those access checks are not endorsements or independent replication. The selected source set includes multiple reports from the same organization; repeated references to the same source or incident are not independent corroboration. Some publishers may restrict access in your location.
