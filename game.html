Controls, saves and source boundaries

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys; drag to orbit, Q/E to turn, and Enter to interact near a station. Touch controls and an equivalent district travel list are available. Escape pauses or closes a panel.

Progress is saved on this browser only. Storage failures are labeled session-only. The campaign never writes to your existing planning checklist or watchlist. The complete book and individually mapped forecasts remain accessible through the field archive.
